# Mentoring and Community

## Overview
Records Elena's mentoring activities and community involvement that span across roles. These are used to support claims about leadership, mentoring, and culture contribution on CVs.

## Ongoing Activities

### Internal mentoring
- **Aegon UK (2022–present):** Mentors 2 mid-level frontend engineers informally (not through a formal programme). Meets fortnightly for 30-minute sessions focused on career development and technical growth. One mentee has since been promoted to senior.
- **Kova (2020–2022):** Participated in Kova's formal mentoring programme. Mentored 1 junior engineer over 12 months.

### Women in Tech Bristol
- **2019–present:** Active member of the Women in Tech Bristol meetup group. Attended regularly since moving to Bristol. Has given one talk (2021, "From Backend to Frontend: A Non-Linear Career Path"). Helps organise events occasionally but is not a committee member.

### Code First Girls
- **2023–2024:** Volunteered as an instructor for one cohort of the Code First Girls "Introduction to Web Development" course (8 weeks, 2 hours/week). Taught HTML, CSS, and introductory JavaScript to 15 women and non-binary learners.

## Positioning notes
Elena's mentoring and community involvement is genuine but modest. She is not a prolific community organiser or a high-profile diversity advocate. The mentoring is primarily one-to-one and informal. This should be surfaced as evidence of investment in others' growth and community participation, not as a defining characteristic of her career. It is most relevant for roles that value culture contribution, mentoring capability, or diversity awareness.
