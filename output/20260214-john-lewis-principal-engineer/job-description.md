# Principal Engineer — Web Modernisation

**Company:** John Lewis Partnership
**Location:** London, UK (Victoria) or Bracknell — Hybrid, 2 days/week in office
**Salary:** £125,000 – £150,000 + Partnership bonus

## About the Role

John Lewis Partnership is undertaking a significant modernisation of its digital platforms. Our customer-facing web experiences for both John Lewis and Waitrose are built on a legacy frontend stack that is increasingly difficult to maintain, extend, and hire for. We need a Principal Engineer to lead the technical strategy and execution of migrating our web frontends to a modern React-based architecture.

This is a critical role within our Technology division. You'll work across multiple teams to design the migration approach, establish new architectural foundations, and coordinate execution across a large engineering organisation.

## What You'll Do

- Lead the technical strategy for migrating John Lewis and Waitrose web frontends from our legacy stack (Angular 1.x / AngularJS and server-rendered templates) to React and TypeScript
- Design an incremental migration approach that avoids feature freezes and minimises risk to the live customer experience
- Establish the target frontend architecture, including component libraries, state management patterns, build tooling, and deployment pipelines
- Coordinate migration execution across 6+ product teams (~30 frontend engineers)
- Build and maintain shared migration tooling, codemods, and documentation
- Work with UX and Design to establish a unified design system for the new platform
- Set frontend engineering standards and best practices across the organisation
- Report progress to senior technology leadership and the Partnership Board as needed
- Mentor and upskill engineers in modern frontend technologies

## What We're Looking For

### Essential

- Significant experience as a Principal Engineer or equivalent senior technical leader in a large organisation
- Proven track record of leading large-scale frontend migrations (e.g. Angular to React, jQuery to React, or similar)
- Deep expertise in React and TypeScript
- Experience designing incremental migration strategies that avoid "big bang" rewrites
- Experience with micro-frontend architectures or modular frontend patterns
- Experience building and scaling design systems or component libraries
- Understanding of frontend build tooling, bundlers, and CI/CD pipelines
- Experience working with and influencing large numbers of engineers (20+) across multiple teams without direct authority
- Strong written and verbal communication skills
- Comfortable operating in complex organisational environments with competing priorities

### Desirable

- Experience with codemods or automated code transformation tools (jscodeshift, ts-morph, or similar)
- Experience in retail, e-commerce, or consumer-facing digital platforms
- Experience with Angular/AngularJS (useful for understanding the migration source)
- Experience with Next.js or server-side React rendering
- Knowledge of web accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1/2.2)
- Experience with monorepo tooling (Nx, Turborepo)
- Conference speaking or published writing on migration or frontend architecture topics
