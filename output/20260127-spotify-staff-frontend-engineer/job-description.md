# Staff Frontend Engineer — Web Platform

**Company:** Spotify
**Location:** London, UK (Hybrid — 2 days/week in office, Adelphi Building)
**Team:** Web Platform

## About the Role

We're looking for a Staff Frontend Engineer to join our Web Platform team in London. This team is responsible for the foundational web infrastructure that powers Spotify's desktop and web player experiences, serving hundreds of millions of users worldwide.

As a Staff Engineer, you'll shape the technical direction of our web platform, working across multiple teams to improve developer experience, performance, and reliability. You'll be a key technical leader within the Web Platform organisation, driving architectural decisions and mentoring senior engineers.

## What You'll Do

- Define and drive the technical strategy for Spotify's web platform infrastructure
- Lead large-scale frontend modernisation initiatives across multiple teams and codebases
- Design and build shared tooling, libraries, and frameworks used by 100+ frontend engineers
- Establish and evolve frontend architectural standards across the organisation
- Collaborate with product teams to understand their needs and build platform capabilities that unblock them
- Mentor and grow senior engineers, contributing to a strong engineering culture
- Evaluate and adopt new technologies, balancing innovation with stability
- Work closely with design systems, developer experience, and backend platform teams

## Who You Are

- You have 10+ years of professional software engineering experience, with deep expertise in frontend/web technologies
- You have experience as a Staff Engineer (or equivalent senior IC role) at a technology company
- You have a track record of leading large-scale frontend architecture or platform initiatives across multiple teams
- You are an expert in TypeScript and React, with strong opinions on how to build maintainable, performant web applications
- You have experience with build systems, bundlers, and frontend tooling (Webpack, Vite, or similar)
- You have experience with design systems and component libraries at scale
- You understand micro-frontend architectures and their trade-offs
- You are comfortable working in a large, distributed engineering organisation
- You communicate complex technical concepts clearly, both in writing and in person
- You have a bias for action and pragmatic decision-making

## Nice to Have

- Experience with monorepo tooling (Nx, Turborepo, Bazel)
- Experience with server-side rendering or streaming architectures
- Contributions to open-source projects
- Experience with performance optimisation at scale (Core Web Vitals, bundle analysis)
- Experience with A/B testing infrastructure
