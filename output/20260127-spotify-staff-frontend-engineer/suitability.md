- **Cross-cutting role:** The Web Platform team sits across multiple product teams, building shared infrastructure. This matches Elena's strong preference for cross-cutting or platform roles rather than being embedded in a single product team.
- **Autonomy:** "Define and drive the technical strategy" signals high autonomy and the ability to shape technical direction, which is a strong preference.
- **Migration and modernisation:** "Lead large-scale frontend modernisation initiatives" is directly aligned with Elena's core professional narrative and strongest experience.

- **Benefits:** RSUs in a publicly listed company (SPOT), 25 days leave, private medical, pension matched to 6%, learning budget, and 6 months parental leave. These all align well with Elena's preferences.
