# Suitability Report: Lead Frontend Developer — Government Digital Service

## Overall

- **Fit score:** Poor fit
- **Recommendation:** Skip
- **One-line summary:** Strong technical and domain alignment, but the salary (£68k-£78k) is roughly half Elena's £130k minimum, making this a dealbreaker regardless of other factors.

## Key Alignments

- **Direct GDS experience:** Elena worked at GDS from 2016-2018 as a Frontend Developer, contributing to the GOV.UK Design System and building with progressive enhancement and WCAG 2.1 AA standards. This is the exact team and codebase referenced in the JD.
- **Design systems expertise:** Elena has built and maintained design systems at three organisations (Wellframe, Kova, Aegon), scaling from 45 to 80+ components. The GOV.UK Design System work is directly relevant.
- **Accessibility depth:** Strong WCAG foundations from GDS, reinforced at every subsequent role. Led accessibility audits, built automated WCAG compliance testing into CI at Aegon, and built a personal accessibility audit dashboard. Has given a talk specifically on accessibility lessons from GDS.
- **Progressive enhancement:** GDS instilled this discipline, and Elena has maintained it throughout her career. Few candidates at this level will have this foundation combined with modern React experience.
- **Node.js and server-side rendering:** Experience with Node.js/Express and Nunjucks templating from the original GDS role. Ongoing Node.js usage for build tooling and development servers.
- **Component libraries and open source:** Has contributed to the GOV.UK Design System previously, and maintains an open-source migration tool (react-migrator, 340 GitHub stars).
- **Line management experience:** Currently leads a 3-person migration accelerator team at Aegon, which maps to the JD's requirement for managing 4 frontend developers.
- **Hands-on technical leadership:** Elena's current role combines architectural ownership with writing production code, which matches the JD's description of a hands-on leadership role.
- **Cross-disciplinary collaboration:** Extensive experience working with interaction designers, content designers, and user researchers (at GDS directly, and in multidisciplinary teams since).
- **Conference speaking:** Has spoken at React Summit and Frontend Connect, aligning with the JD's requirement to represent GDS at external events.
- **Government/public sector interest:** Listed as a nice-to-have industry in Elena's preferences, and she has direct prior experience here.

## Key Concerns

- **Salary is a dealbreaker:** The role pays £68,000-£78,000. Elena's absolute minimum is £130,000. Even accounting for the 28.97% Civil Service pension (which is genuinely valuable, adding roughly £20k-£23k in employer contribution), the total compensation package falls well short. This gap is not negotiable within Civil Service pay bands.
- **Seniority mismatch:** Grade 7 is a mid-level management grade in the Civil Service. Elena is currently a Principal Engineer leading migration across 15 applications and 45+ developers. This role would represent a significant step down in scope, influence, and seniority.
- **Tech stack mismatch (hard requirement):** The JD does not mention React or TypeScript. The GOV.UK Design System frontend stack is Nunjucks, vanilla JavaScript, and server-rendered HTML. Elena's hard requirement is that the role must be React and TypeScript primarily. While the Design System is evolving, the current stack does not meet this criterion.
- **London hybrid requirement:** The role requires minimum 2 days per week in the Whitechapel office. Elena's preference is fully remote, with hybrid acceptable only in Bristol or locations within easy reach of Bristol (e.g., near Paddington). Whitechapel is not close to Paddington, adding commute friction on top of the Bristol-to-London journey.
- **Scope reduction:** Moving from coordinating 8 teams and 45 developers to leading 4 developers would represent a significant narrowing of influence and challenge.

## Experience Highlights

If Elena were to apply despite the concerns above, these would be the strongest points to emphasise:

- **Direct GOV.UK Design System contribution experience** from the 2016-2018 GDS role, including component contributions and accessibility audit work.
- **Design system creation and scaling** across three organisations (60+ components at Wellframe, 45 at Kova, 80+ at Aegon), demonstrating the ability to build and maintain component libraries at varying scales.
- **Accessibility expertise spanning career:** GDS foundations, WCAG audits, automated compliance testing in CI, personal a11y-audit-dashboard project, and a public talk on accessibility lessons from GDS.
- **Line management of a small technical team** at Aegon (3 direct reports), showing readiness for the 4-person team described in the JD.
- **Conference speaking** at React Summit and Frontend Connect, relevant to the requirement to represent GDS externally.
- **Progressive enhancement discipline** from GDS, carried through to all subsequent roles.

## Questions to Ask

These questions would be relevant if Elena were considering the role despite the salary gap:

- What is the technology direction for GOV.UK Frontend? Is there any planned adoption of React, TypeScript, or modern component frameworks, or will the stack remain Nunjucks and vanilla JavaScript?
- What does the day-to-day split look like between writing code, reviewing PRs, line management, and cross-government community work?
- Is there any flexibility on grade or salary for candidates with significantly more experience than the role typically attracts?
- How much autonomy does the Lead have in shaping the Design System's technical direction versus following existing GDS technology strategy?

## Compensation Note

The salary of £68,000-£78,000 is a clear dealbreaker. Elena's minimum base salary is £130,000, and Civil Service Grade 7 pay bands do not offer the flexibility to bridge a gap of this magnitude. The 28.97% Civil Service pension is genuinely excellent and adds significant value, but even when factored in, the total package remains substantially below Elena's requirements. This role should be skipped on compensation grounds alone.
