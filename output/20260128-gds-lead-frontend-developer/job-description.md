# Lead Frontend Developer

**Company:** Government Digital Service (GDS)
**Location:** London, UK (Whitechapel) — Hybrid, minimum 2 days/week in office
**Grade:** Grade 7
**Salary:** £68,000 – £78,000 (London weighting included)
**Contract:** Permanent, full-time

## About the Role

The Government Digital Service is looking for a Lead Frontend Developer to join the Design System team. You'll lead the frontend engineering direction of the GOV.UK Design System, one of the most widely used design systems in the world, adopted by hundreds of government services across the UK and internationally.

This is a hands-on technical leadership role. You'll write code, review pull requests, and shape the architectural direction of the Design System's frontend codebase, while also leading a team of 4 frontend developers.

## Responsibilities

- Lead the frontend engineering direction of the GOV.UK Design System
- Line manage a team of 4 frontend developers, supporting their growth and development
- Make architectural decisions about the Design System's frontend technology choices
- Write production code, review pull requests, and maintain high code quality standards
- Champion progressive enhancement, accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA), and inclusive design in all technical decisions
- Work closely with interaction designers, content designers, and user researchers to develop new patterns and components
- Engage with the cross-government frontend community, supporting adoption and gathering feedback
- Represent GDS at external events and conferences
- Contribute to GDS's broader technology strategy

## Essential Skills and Experience

- Significant experience leading frontend development teams
- Expert knowledge of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- Deep understanding of progressive enhancement and why it matters for government services
- Strong experience with accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1/2.2) and assistive technology testing
- Experience designing and maintaining component libraries or design systems
- Experience with Node.js and server-side rendering
- Understanding of browser compatibility challenges across a wide range of devices and connections
- Excellent communication skills, both written and verbal
- Experience line managing developers

## Desirable Skills and Experience

- Previous experience working in government or the public sector
- Experience contributing to or maintaining open-source projects
- Knowledge of the GOV.UK Design System, GOV.UK Frontend, or GOV.UK Prototype Kit
- Experience with Nunjucks or similar templating languages
- Understanding of the Service Standard and GDS design principles
