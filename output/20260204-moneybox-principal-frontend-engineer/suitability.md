# Suitability Report: Moneybox — Principal Frontend Engineer

## Overall

- **Fit score:** Cautious fit
- **Recommendation:** Cautious
- **One-line summary:** Strong technical alignment on React/TypeScript and principal-level seniority, but the greenfield startup nature, heavy hiring expectations, below-minimum salary floor, 3-day London hybrid requirement, and pre-IPO equity all run counter to Elena's core preferences.

## Key Alignments

- **Tech stack is a direct match:** React, TypeScript, and Next.js are central to the role. Elena has deep, evidenced expertise in all three.
- **Principal-level seniority:** The title and scope match Elena's target seniority. She would be the most senior frontend engineer, which aligns with her preference for architectural ownership.
- **Fintech domain:** Financial services is a preferred industry, and Elena has direct experience in insurance, pensions, and banking.
- **High autonomy:** Defining web strategy "from the ground up" and owning the technical roadmap suggests strong autonomy and architectural ownership.
- **Consumer-facing product with UX/performance focus:** Elena has demonstrated experience building performant, accessible consumer-facing applications.
- **Working directly with CPO and Head of Design:** Cross-functional senior leadership collaboration aligns with Elena's preference for strategic IC influence.
- **Nice-to-haves are strong:** Elena has experience with regulated industries (Aegon, GDS), React Native is not claimed but the mobile-first-to-web expansion maps well to her platform thinking, and she has limited but real GraphQL experience (Apollo Client at Kova).

## Key Concerns

- **Greenfield, not modernisation:** This is explicitly a "build from the ground up" role. Elena's strengths and preferences are in modernisation, migration, and bringing order to existing systems. Pure greenfield startup work is listed as an explicitly unwanted signal in her preferences. While she can certainly architect a new system, this does not play to what she finds most engaging.
- **Heavy hiring and management focus:** The role expects hiring and growing a team to 6 engineers by year end. Elena has led a 3-person team and has been clear that she is not a full-time engineering manager. Building and managing a 6-person team would represent a significant increase in management responsibility and likely push the role away from hands-on IC work.
- **Salary range partially below minimum:** The range is £120,000-£140,000. Elena's hard minimum is £130,000. The bottom of the range is £10,000 below her floor. She would need to negotiate to at least £130,000, and ideally toward the top of the range given the London commute costs.
- **3 days per week in London office (Fenchurch Street):** Elena strongly prefers fully remote. If hybrid, she prefers Bristol-area or London locations within 10 minutes' walk of the Temple Meads coach to Paddington route. Fenchurch Street is not on that direct route, adding commute complexity. Three days per week is above her preferred maximum of 2 days, and the Bristol-to-London commute would be costly and time-consuming.
- **Pre-IPO stock options (EMI scheme):** Elena has explicitly stated that stock options for pre-IPO companies do not factor into compensation comparisons. The EMI scheme should be treated as having zero value for decision-making purposes.
- **No pension contribution mentioned:** The benefits list does not mention employer pension contribution. Pension is flagged as important in Elena's preferences.
- **Startup pace and ambiguity:** The JD emphasises "fast-paced startup environment with ambiguity." Elena prefers established companies (Series C+) and structured environments, though she has worked at a Series C fintech (Kova). Moneybox appears to be a later-stage startup (over £100m raised, 1m+ users), which mitigates this somewhat.
- **Risk of being sidelined into management:** With the expectation to hire 6 engineers and define team processes, there is a meaningful risk that this role evolves into a full-time engineering management position, which Elena explicitly does not want.

## Experience Highlights

If Elena decides to pursue this role, the following experience should be emphasised:

- **Kova (Staff Frontend Engineer):** Most directly relevant. First Staff-level engineer at a Series C fintech, providing cross-team architectural direction for multiple React applications. Built micro-frontend architecture, founded a design system, drove TypeScript adoption, established performance monitoring. Demonstrates ability to define frontend strategy in a scaling fintech.
- **Aegon UK (Principal, both roles):** Demonstrates principal-level architectural leadership at scale, design system creation (80+ components), and the ability to establish engineering standards across multiple teams. The component library and tooling work is highly relevant even though the migration context is less applicable.
- **a11y-audit-dashboard (personal project):** Built with Next.js App Router, React Server Components, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS, directly demonstrating currency with the Moneybox tech stack.
- **Wellframe Health:** Consumer-facing React application with performance optimisation (load time reduction from 8-10s to under 2s), component library creation, and accessibility improvements. Relevant to the "high standards for UX and performance" requirement.
- **GDS:** Accessibility foundations and contributing to a design system used by hundreds of services. Relevant to the "consumer-facing products" and quality standards expectations.
- **Conference speaking:** React Summit talk demonstrates thought leadership and public communication skills relevant to a principal-level hire.

## Questions to Ask

These questions would materially change the assessment:

- **What is the expected split between hands-on engineering and management/hiring?** If the role is primarily management by month 6, it is likely a poor fit. If Elena can remain mostly hands-on with hiring as a secondary responsibility, it becomes more viable.
- **Is the 3-day office requirement negotiable?** Could this be reduced to 2 days, or could some days be worked from a Bristol co-working space?
- **What is the employer pension contribution?** This is not mentioned in the JD and is important to Elena.
- **What is the realistic meeting load?** A principal role with hiring responsibility and cross-team coordination could easily exceed Elena's 1/3 maximum.
- **Is the salary negotiable toward the top of the range?** Elena needs at least £130,000 and ideally more given London commute costs.
- **How established is the backend/mobile engineering culture?** Understanding the existing engineering maturity would clarify whether this is truly "greenfield with no systems context" or whether there are established patterns to build upon.
- **What happens to this role once the team is built?** Does the Principal remain hands-on, or does it transition into an Engineering Manager position?

## Compensation Note

The salary range of £120,000-£140,000 partially overlaps with Elena's £130,000 hard minimum. The bottom of the range is a dealbreaker. Elena should clarify early that she requires at least £130,000 base salary and that the EMI share options do not factor into her compensation assessment. The absence of a stated pension contribution is a concern that should be raised in the first conversation. Given the 3-day London commute from Bristol, a salary toward the top of the range (£140,000) would be more appropriate to offset travel costs.
