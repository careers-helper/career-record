# Principal Frontend Engineer

**Company:** Moneybox
**Location:** London, UK — Hybrid (3 days/week in office, Fenchurch Street)
**Salary:** £120,000 – £140,000

## About Moneybox

Moneybox is a savings and investing app that helps over 1 million people build their financial future. We make it easy to save, invest, and manage money through our award-winning mobile app. We're backed by leading investors and have raised over £100m in funding.

## About the Role

We're hiring a Principal Frontend Engineer to lead the technical direction of our web platform. Our web presence is growing rapidly: we're building a full-featured web dashboard for customers, a new partner portal for financial advisers, and expanding our marketing site.

This is a new role created as part of our growth from a mobile-first company into a multi-platform experience. You'll be our most senior frontend engineer and will define the web strategy from the ground up.

## What You'll Do

- Define the frontend architecture for Moneybox's web platform (greenfield)
- Build a new React + Next.js web dashboard from scratch, working closely with mobile and backend teams
- Establish frontend engineering standards, tooling, and best practices
- Hire and mentor a growing frontend team (target: 6 engineers by end of year)
- Own the technical roadmap for all web properties
- Work directly with the CPO and Head of Design on product direction
- Evaluate build-vs-buy decisions for shared infrastructure
- Drive adoption of TypeScript across web properties

## Who You Are

- 12+ years of software engineering experience with deep frontend expertise
- Proven experience as a Principal Engineer or equivalent senior technical leader
- Expert in React and TypeScript; strong Next.js experience required
- Experience building consumer-facing products with high standards for UX and performance
- Experience hiring, mentoring, and growing frontend teams
- Strong opinions on testing strategy, CI/CD, and code quality
- Comfortable operating in a fast-paced startup environment with ambiguity
- Excellent product sense and ability to make pragmatic technical decisions

## Nice to Have

- Experience with mobile-first companies expanding to web
- Experience with financial services or regulated industries
- Experience with React Native or cross-platform development
- Knowledge of SEO and web marketing optimisation
- Experience with GraphQL
