# Suitability Report: Staff Frontend Engineer — BBC iPlayer & Sounds

## Overall

- **Fit score:** Poor fit
- **Recommendation:** Skip
- **One-line summary:** Strong technical alignment on React, TypeScript, accessibility, and design systems, but the role fails on two hard requirements: location (Salford, 3 days/week, no remote option) and salary (£75k-£95k, well below the £130k minimum).

## Key Alignments

- **Technology stack:** React and TypeScript primary, which matches Elena's core expertise exactly.
- **Seniority and role type:** Staff Engineer at a large organisation with hands-on technical leadership across multiple squads, aligning well with Elena's preferred IC-with-influence model.
- **Design systems and component libraries:** Architecting and maintaining shared component libraries across products directly mirrors Elena's work at Aegon (80+ component library) and Kova (45-component design system).
- **Accessibility:** BBC's WCAG 2.2 AA requirement and commitment to inclusive design aligns with Elena's GDS foundations and ongoing accessibility work (a11y-audit-dashboard, axe-core integration in CI at Aegon).
- **Web performance:** Core Web Vitals optimisation is a strong match, given Elena's performance work at Kova (Lighthouse CI, performance budgets) and Wellframe (dashboard load time reduction).
- **Mentoring:** The role involves developing frontend engineers across the team, which aligns with Elena's mentoring track record (migration accelerator team, informal mentoring at Aegon, formal mentoring at Kova).
- **Server-side rendering:** Elena has direct Next.js experience through the a11y-audit-dashboard project and GDS server-rendering background.
- **Industry:** Public sector/mission-driven organisation is listed as a nice-to-have preference, and the BBC fits this category.
- **Scale:** Millions of daily users across iPlayer and Sounds would represent high-traffic consumer product experience, which Elena has not had at this scale but has relevant foundations for.

## Key Concerns

- **Location (dealbreaker):** The role is based in Salford (MediaCityUK), requiring 3 days per week in the office with no fully remote option. Elena is based in Bristol. Salford is not within reasonable commuting distance, and the job description explicitly states no remote working. This fails the location preference outright: it is neither Bristol-area, London-accessible, nor remote.
- **Salary (dealbreaker):** The advertised range of £75,000-£95,000 is significantly below Elena's £130,000 minimum. Even at the top of the band, there is a £35,000 shortfall. BBC salary bands tend to be rigid, and public sector pay structures make negotiation above the stated band unlikely.
- **Title regression:** The role is Staff Engineer, whereas Elena's current title is Principal. While Staff at a large organisation like the BBC is within the acceptable range, it represents a step back in title from her current position.
- **Media streaming experience:** The desirable experience includes media streaming and video/audio web technologies. Elena has no experience in this domain.
- **Micro-frontend architecture:** Listed as desirable. Elena has strong micro-frontend experience (module federation at Kova), so this is actually a positive, though the JD frames it as optional.
- **Pension:** BBC pension (employer contribution up to 10%) is good but does not compensate for the salary gap.

## Experience Highlights

If applying despite the dealbreakers, Elena should emphasise:

- **Design systems at scale:** The Aegon React UI component library (80+ components, design tokens, WCAG compliance in CI, used by 8 teams) and Kova UI (45 components, Storybook, Chromatic) directly address the shared component library requirement.
- **React and TypeScript depth:** Current daily use across a 15-application estate, combined with TypeScript migration leadership at Kova (0% to 85% coverage).
- **Accessibility credentials:** GDS background (WCAG 2.1 AA as a hard requirement, screen reader testing, Design System contributions), plus the a11y-audit-dashboard personal project and automated WCAG compliance testing at Aegon.
- **Web performance:** Lighthouse CI and Core Web Vitals monitoring at Kova, clinician dashboard performance overhaul at Wellframe.
- **Cross-team technical leadership:** Influencing 45 developers across 8 teams at Aegon without positional authority closely mirrors leading 15 engineers across 3 squads at the BBC.
- **Server-side rendering:** Next.js experience through the a11y-audit-dashboard, plus GDS server-rendered service experience.
- **Community of practice:** The Aegon frontend community of practice (grown from 12 to 35+ engineers) demonstrates the kind of cross-team culture building the BBC role would require.

## Questions to Ask

These questions would be relevant if the dealbreakers could somehow be resolved:

- Is there any flexibility on the hybrid arrangement for candidates based outside the Manchester/Salford area?
- What is the realistic meeting load for this role? The JD mentions collaboration with UX, product, and backend teams, which could push meeting time above the preferred 20% threshold.
- How is the team structured? Is this a cross-cutting platform role, or is the Staff Engineer embedded within one of the 3 squads?
- What is the current state of the frontend architecture? Is there modernisation or migration work involved, or is it primarily feature delivery on an established stack?
- Is there any scope for the role to evolve toward Principal level?

## Compensation Note

The advertised salary range of £75,000-£95,000 is a clear miss against Elena's £130,000 minimum. BBC Band D salary structures are typically non-negotiable beyond the stated range. The pension (up to 10% employer contribution) and annual bonus are positive but do not bridge a £35,000+ gap. Combined with the relocation/commuting costs that the Salford location would require, the total compensation picture is substantially below Elena's requirements. This is a dealbreaker that should be confirmed before investing time in an application.
