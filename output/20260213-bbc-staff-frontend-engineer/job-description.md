# Staff Frontend Engineer — iPlayer & Sounds

**Company:** BBC
**Location:** Salford, UK (MediaCityUK) — Hybrid, 3 days/week in office
**Band:** Band D (Senior Leadership)
**Salary:** £75,000 – £95,000

## About the Role

BBC iPlayer and BBC Sounds are two of the UK's most-loved digital products, serving millions of users daily across web, mobile, and connected TV platforms. We're looking for a Staff Frontend Engineer to join the Web Engineering team within the iPlayer & Sounds division.

You'll provide technical leadership for the web experiences of both products, working with a team of 15 frontend engineers across 3 squads. This is a hands-on role: you'll write code, lead architectural decisions, and drive improvements to our frontend platform.

## What You'll Do

- Provide technical leadership for iPlayer and Sounds web experiences
- Lead the evolution of our React-based single-page applications serving millions of daily users
- Drive performance optimisation across both products (Core Web Vitals, streaming performance, accessibility)
- Architect and maintain shared component libraries used across iPlayer, Sounds, and other BBC products
- Lead adoption of modern frontend tooling and practices across the team
- Collaborate with UX, product, and backend teams to deliver outstanding user experiences
- Mentor and develop frontend engineers across the team
- Contribute to BBC-wide frontend standards and the BBC Design System
- Ensure all web experiences meet BBC accessibility standards (WCAG 2.2 AA) and work across a wide range of devices

## Essential Experience

- Extensive experience in frontend engineering with deep expertise in React and TypeScript
- Track record as a Staff Engineer or equivalent technical leader
- Experience leading frontend architecture for high-traffic consumer products
- Strong understanding of web performance optimisation and measurement
- Experience with server-side rendering (Next.js or similar)
- Deep commitment to accessibility and inclusive design
- Experience with design systems and shared component libraries
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills
- Experience mentoring and growing engineers

## Desirable Experience

- Experience with media streaming or video/audio web technologies
- Experience with micro-frontend architectures
- Knowledge of Progressive Web Apps and offline-first patterns
- Experience with A/B testing and experimentation platforms
- Experience working in a large, distributed engineering organisation
- Public sector or mission-driven organisation experience
